## Decisions

- NUCLEO-F446RE remains the low-level controller.
- ESP32-S3 remains the support controller, not the motor controller.
- MDD10A is the first motor driver path.
- BTS7960 is superseded for the first drivetrain path.
- STM32 owns motor output permission and safety gate.
- UART/USB serial is first; CAN is required later.
- FreeRTOS comes after HAL bare-metal drivetrain validation.
- LL Driver migration comes after a known-good HAL baseline.
- ROS 2 bridge comes after low-level drivetrain safety is validated.
