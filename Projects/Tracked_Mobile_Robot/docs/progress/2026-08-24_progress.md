## Decisions and Design Audit

- Direct `PC7-GND` is a temporary firmware stimulus only. It must be removed before the
  VO617A-3 path test because leaving it installed bypasses the sense path.
- F2 procurement candidate is now Littelfuse `0287001.PXCN` 1 A ATOF with
  `FHAC0001ZXJA`; it protects the low-current control branch, not the motor branch. Both are pending
  incoming inspection.
- The 6P harness is reserved for three independent two-wire loops: S0-A NC, S0-B NC and S2 NO.
  Motor current, PC7 itself, K1/K2 bodies and F2 do not pass through this connector. Actual cavity
  numbering waits for received housing marks and 100% continuity/isolation mapping.
- A design audit found a real limitation in the current RevB K2 self-hold topology. If S2 is stuck
  closed or its 6P pair is shorted, releasing S0 or restoring power can energize K2/K1 automatically.
  The no-auto-reenable claim is therefore valid only when S2 is healthy/released and there is no
  cross-short.
- Firmware `DISARMED/PWM=0` is an independent safety layer, not a substitute for hardware
  motor-rail no-auto-reenable. `FM-ESTOP-014` mitigation remains open, and the present circuit is not
  claimed as single-fault tolerant or industrial safety rated.
